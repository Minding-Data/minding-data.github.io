Memory

Contextual Dynamics LabDartmouth College

In the Contextual Dynamics Lab, we study how memory works and what its limits are, using computational models and brain recordings. My focused interest is in learning, memory, and forgetting- particularly how what is learned and what is lost shape trauma-related disorders like PTSD and CPTSD.

Related observational work on childhood adversity is on the Adversity, Social, and Legal Psych page.
