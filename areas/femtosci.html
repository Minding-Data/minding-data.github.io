sarah.kerns@femtosci.com

Ms. Kerns guides the biological and chemical applications of polycrystalline and single-crystal diamond technologies at FemtoSci and shapes human factors-informed design. Working closely with the engineering team, she bridges the company’s decades of experience in materials engineering/microfabrication with the development of agile, highly adaptable solutions suited to the practical demands of real-world use.

Her technical background in physical and organic chemistry, chemical synthesis, and biological and chemical imaging is supported by her foundational research designing, synthesizing and evaluating innovative functionalization of diamond nanoparticles. Her work in biochemistry and microbiology, including the evaluation of pathogen-inhibitory compounds in drug development, provides a foundation for expanding platforms into biological threat detection. This wet-lab experience is complemented by work in computational modeling and large-scale multivariate dataset analysis.

A U.S. Army veteran and former Emergency Medical Technician (EMT), Ms. Kerns holds degrees in Biochemistry and Psychology from Wellesley College and a Master’s in Cognitive Neuroscience from Dartmouth College. Her training in neuroscience and psychology, combined with her field-response experience, enables her to anticipate perceptual and cognitive sources of operator error, particularly under demanding conditions. Her expertise in data visualization and scientific communication further supports the effective dissemination of complex research findings to scientific, technical, and interdisciplinary audiences.
