Childhood Adversity

Observational research on childhood adversity.

This includes a 2022 ISTSS poster with Laura Germine, Exploring the childhood trauma monolith: A large-scale multivariate analysis of childhood adversity (published as a poster; no public URL listed). Related consulting with TestMyBrain.org, beginning 2022, involved large-scale online studies of childhood adversity and the dynamic flanker task.

Separate, upcoming experimental work on trauma and memory is described on the Memory page.
