Childhood Adversity

My observational work on childhood adversity examines whether its many forms belong together. I presented a 2022 ISTSS poster, Exploring the childhood trauma monolith: A large-scale multivariate analysis of childhood adversity. Using data collected on TestMyBrain.org over a ten-year period via the retrospective Childhood Adversity Questionnaire, I found that most types of childhood abuse clustered together and related similarly to socioeconomic status, but sexual abuse stood apart. Additional graphs, the questionnaire, and methodological notes are linked from the poster page. Separate, upcoming experimental work on trauma and memory is described on the Memory page.

Social Psych

As a research assistant in the Akert lab at Wellesley College (2018-2019), I worked on in-group and prejudice. This includes my unpublished Fall 2018 psychology capstone, Public Comment, Private Blame: The Impact of Social Media on Group-Based Opinion, which asked to what extent in-group favoritism could outweigh public praise and criticism of a group, and whether the author's group membership altered that effect. I presented the work in a 2019 Ruhlman Conference panel.

Legal Psych

As a research assistant in the Cheek Lab at Wellesley College (2017-2018), I conducted guided independent research on narcissism, manipulation, and abuse, and a practicum on psychological abuse and its treatment in the courts, with courtroom-based observational research at Middlesex County Courts in Natick, MA.
