Face Perception

Prosopometamorphopsia · Face and object processing

sarah.kerns@wellesley.edu

Face and object processing, prosopometamorphopsia (face distortions), and neuropsychology, psychophysics, and neuroimaging in special populations.

Published work in this area includes a 2026 Cognitive Neuropsychology case study of early-emerging prosopometamorphopsia and a 2025 master’s thesis establishing face-specific and general-distortion prosopometamorphopsia types. Much of the published clinical and neuroimaging work listed below was done with the Social Perception Lab (Duchaine Lab) at Dartmouth College.

In progress Dissociating Face and Ovoid Processing in the Fusiform Face Area Using Prosopometamorphopsia, manuscript in draft (secondary data collection).
