Face Perception

Prosopometamorphopsia · Face and object processing

sarah.kerns@wellesley.edu

My master’s research studied face and object processing, including work with the special prosopometamorphopsia (PMO) population while at the Social Perception Lab/Dartmouth College. In my 2025 master’s thesis, I developed a new test and established face-specific and general-distortion PMO types. My data have since contributed to multiple conference posters and a 2026 Cognitive Neuropsychology case study of early-emerging PMO.
