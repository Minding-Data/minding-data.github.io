Teaching Design and Development

Curriculum development manager and content creator for the TeLLab2 online teaching platform for data skills and analysis (Wellesley College; tellab.org), 2020–2022, including a pedagogical framework, teaching modules in experimental design and data visualization (Stroop, Flanker, and reasoning tasks), experimental paradigms, and grant preparation.

Related work on assessment design, as a consultant in data visualization and measure design with Educational Testing Service (ETS) (2026), is on the DataViz page.

Hands-on Teaching

K–13 teacher and curriculum developer, with decades of teaching experience and an expertise in finding tripping points and fixing them.

Teaching Assistant, PSYC 11: Laboratory in Psychological Science, Dartmouth College, Spring 2025 and Fall 2025. Undergraduate and postbaccalaureate research mentor at Dartmouth College (2023–2025) and Wellesley College (2026–present).
