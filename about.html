About

Minding Data is the research portfolio of Sarah H. Kerns—bridging neuroscience, data visualization, education, and materials science. Sarah is a U.S. Army veteran, a veteran educator, and an inveterate measure builder, and she'll always tell you if you have spinach in your teeth.
